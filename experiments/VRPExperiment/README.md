# VRPExperiment

One hypothesis: the BTC and ETH variance risk premium, measured from public
option data, is positive and large enough to exceed the measured round-trip
cost of harvesting it on a venue reachable by a British Columbia resident.

This is a measurement, not a sleeve. No capital is deployed at any stage of
the registered ladder. A successor sleeve, if one is ever warranted, requires
its own registration in its own repo.

Successor to ORBExperiment H1 and H1b, both closed cost-bound on a measured
median entry half-spread of 0.0766 USD/share (n = 550).
