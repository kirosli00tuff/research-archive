## Classification

**Canada: PROHIBITED. British Columbia: PROHIBITED, as part of Canada.**

The entry reads **"Canada"** and nothing else. The distinction matters and is
recorded rather than glossed: **five other entries carry qualifiers that narrow
the prohibition** — Belarus and the Russian Federation carry EEA/Switzerland
nationality exemptions; Panama and the United Arab Emirates are restricted only
for *Retail Investors* and only to *spot products*; the United Kingdom is
restricted only for *Retail Clients*; and Ukraine is restricted only in three
named regions. **Canada carries no qualifier of any kind.** It is a bare,
unconditional, whole-country prohibition covering location, incorporation,
establishment and residence.

There is therefore no reading of this policy under which a British Columbia
resident may access or use the Deribit platform or its services. **This is a
measured finding and is not worked around.**
