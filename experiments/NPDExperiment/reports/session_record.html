The carried estimate was wrong

Prior chat estimated a cutoff of 2024-05-31 and a window of 515 trading days. The verified cutoff is four months later and the real window is 432 days. Had the estimate been carried into the registration, the power arithmetic would have been overstated by 19 percent and the guard band would have started inside territory the model had already read.

Power, and the limitation registered beside it

The registered floor is a detectable rank IC of 0.025 at 30 names. The derived window gives 0.01787, so collection was authorized. But the floor is not the number that governs interpretation, and the registration says so before any money was spent.

The primary contrast is a difference of two daily IC series over a five-session horizon. Consecutive start dates share four fifths of their forward window, inflating the variance of the mean by 3.4 and cutting 427 evaluable days to 125.6 effective.

Against a realistic effect band of 0.01 to 0.05, the primary contrast detects the upper half and is underpowered for the lower half. A null on the primary does not distinguish "no effect" from "an effect below 0.033".

The one-session secondary carries no overlap penalty and detects 0.014 to 0.025, which is better. It stays secondary. Promoting the better-powered horizon after seeing a result is precisely the move this repository exists to prevent.

The seal

Forward returns are split by date, and the split is the seal. var/npd_fit.db holds only dates strictly before the window start and is read by the logistic baseline. var/npd_sealed.db holds the window and is read by npd.eval.evaluator and by nothing else. The writer has no reader at all. A guard test walks every source file and fails the moment another module names the sealed store, calls the sealed reader, or adds a read to the writer.

The synthetic run earned its keep

Running the full verdict pipeline end to end on fake returns proved B.2 works without touching one real outcome. It also caught a live defect: daily_ic carried an inverted sign, so a perfect ranking would have scored −1 and the experiment would have reported the exact opposite of what it measured. Fixed, and pinned by a test that asserts the convention.

Anonymization defect, caught before any spend

Two-letter row labels collide with real tickers. KO is Coca-Cola, MA is Mastercard, MU is Micron. On 52 of the 432 window days a randomly drawn label equalled a ticker standing in the same panel, putting that ticker in front of the model.

Panel symbols are now struck from the label pool before any label is drawn. All 432 mappings were rebuilt and the collision count is zero.

A wrong diagnosis, kept on the record

Amendment 3 assumed the format failures were a reasoning-budget problem. The live data refuted it: low failed more often than minimal. Amendment 4 supersedes it.

Amendment 3 stays in the registration rather than being quietly removed. A wrong diagnosis that was acted on is part of the record.

Amendment 5 mattered more than its size

The spend ledger recorded nothing for an attempt whose answer failed to parse. Those calls were billed. Measured across the first 29 gate days it read 0.3494 USD against an actual 0.6429.

A cap enforced against a number 46 percent too low is not a cap, and this is the mechanism the whole spend section leans on.

What the amendments cost, and what they bought

The 26 superseded attempts stay in the ledger and their 0.0286 USD still counts against the cap. Amendment 4 cut the projected total from 9.12 USD to a measured 6.94 USD, cut mean latency from 16.3 seconds to about 3 per attempt, and turned a path heading for BLOCKED into one that fired GO.

The limitation that survives into B.2

The gate ran at minimal reasoning on 813 of 883 attempts. A null result is a null for gpt-5-2025-08-07 at minimal reasoning effort, not for the model at its best. Every sentence written about the outcome carries that qualifier.

53 days carry an answer produced at low after an escalation. Days needing escalation are days where minimal could not hold the format, so if that difficulty correlates with anything about the panel, the sample mixes two configurations non-randomly. The realized effort is stored per day for exactly this reason. B.2 reports the split descriptively, and under Clause 1 it never becomes a verdict.
