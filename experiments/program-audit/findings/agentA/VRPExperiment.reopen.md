## Leads for the cross-repository pass

Every blocker or reopen condition below might be satisfied, bounded, or contradicted by work held in another repository. Best guess of the repository named in each case.

1. **MBT reopen figure (2), the CME exchange-and-regulatory-fee component → MLCryptoEngine.** This repository records MLCryptoEngine's all-in CME round turns ($4.04 MBT, $1.94 MET) against the commission it measured itself ($1.70, $0.40) and infers the difference is CME exchange and regulatory fees — docs/VENUE_TABLE_UPDATE.md:92-96. The residual implies ≈ $1.17 per fill ≈ **1.498 bps of the $7,812.46 MBT notional**, i.e. ≈ 24 bps of the 49.61 bps budget at the p90 count of 16. **Check in MLCryptoEngine whether its round turns in fact decompose into commission + exchange fee**, and at what date and tier. Section 4 forbids carrying the figure into VRPExperiment (REGISTRATION.md:246-247), so this is a cross-repo verification, not a substitution.

It is the programme's best evidence on CME MBT futures cost and bounds the hedge-side leg of the open route.
