## Summary — the strongest case for the thesis in this repository

The verdict VRPExperiment fired (**VENUE_BLOCKED**) survives every recomputation below; nothing here shows HARVESTABLE should have fired, and the registered 2.0× margin test fails on MET at every hedging band from ±0.05 to ±1.00. What does not survive is the strength of the statements built around the verdict. The MET route's deciding sentence — *the commission is larger than the premium*, asserted to be irreversible "because every cost input is a lower bound" — holds only at the ±0.10 hedging band; it reverses at the ±0.20 band the registration itself registered as a mandatory sensitivity and the repository itself measured (central commission-only cost **73.284 bps** against an ETH premium point estimate of **119.052 bps**, n = 1,955), and at every band a $1,000 sleeve could actually execute, because a ±0.10 band asks for a tenth of an indivisible futures contract and the "minimum order increment" REGISTRATION.md:208 required was never recorded. Two of the three `Absent` legs that make HARVESTABLE *structurally* unfireable rest on **four probes, none of which is an authentication refusal** (an HTTP 401 "Invalid Crumb", two "not found" bodies, and one source not attempted). The blocker keeping the last route shut — "obtainable … by nobody without one" — is contradicted by the refusal text the repository itself quotes verbatim, which names a no-account delivery route that was never tried. And the instruction handed to a successor, *do not read the BTC premium as stationary*, rests on the monotone ordering of four point estimates that a heterogeneity test on the repository's own numbers does not reject (Cochran Q = 4.784, 3 df, **p = 0.188**).
