## The strongest case for the thesis, in five lines

This register is unusually disciplined: every headline figure I recomputed reproduced its own artifact exactly, both BLOCKED verdicts are honest negatives decided on measured rates, and the amendments that weaken bars say so in their own headings. The thesis nonetheless holds in a narrow, load-bearing band. (1) The cohort-validity certificate — "the cohort is not survivorship-manufactured" — rests on a six-instrument probe that drew **zero** HIP-3 instruments, while **53** delisted HIP-3 instruments in the repository's own census serve no candle history at all: the registered FAIL condition, realised, never once placed beside the verdict. (3) The amendment that carried the surviving hypothesis over its 0.80 power target adopted an unmeasured interpolated assumption for the survivor, while the register's own stated principle forced the pessimistic end onto the two hypotheses the same cull blocked. (4) Beneath all of it, every verdict statistic is an exact binomial whose exactness is asserted "with no asymptotic approximation anywhere" while the independence it rests on is never stated, never tested, and guaranteed violated by control reuse with replacement and wholly overlapping 30-day windows.
