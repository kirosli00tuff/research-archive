## 5. Ledger notes for the cross-repository pass

- **One bar carries everything.** The band top of 0.08 is the only registered figure that
  turns a cost computation into a "surviving region", it is a round-up of a single
  published figure (7.7 points) whose n and identifier are both absent, and its exploitable
  entry price (88.3c) falls **outside** the region the verdict fires over. Counterfactual
  recomputation is in DRAFT-p1-register-1.
- **The evidence standard is asymmetric by construction, not by intent.** ADR-012 governs
  claims about *permission* only. Every negative in this repository was decided under it;
  the one positive-flavoured verdict was decided against literature figures to which no
  comparable standard (and not even standing rule 4's "verify every external figure live")
  was applied.
- **Cross-repository figures this repository imports and relies on:** NPDExperiment's
  realized MDE **0.0512** against a 0.01–0.05 band after **415 days** (REGISTRATION.md:29-44;
  CLAUDE.md:54-58); InsiderExperiment's **BLOCKED at 49.35 bp** (:46-47); MLCryptoEngine's
  H2 cross-venue transfer at **AUC 0.501** (HYPOTHESES.md:103), which is the load-bearing
  ground for H1t being UNTESTED and is carried in six places. None of the three carries its
  own n inside this repository. If any of those figures is itself in question, H1t's
  UNTESTED status and the README's NPD/Insider framing move with it.
- **Dates.** Amendments 1–3 and DECISIONS.md's second ADR-006/ADR-007 pair plus ADR-008 are
  stamped 2026-08-30 but were committed 2026-08-29T18:05:26Z (`f686036`). Any cross-repo
  timeline built from this repository's document stamps rather than its commits will be
  wrong by a day for those six entries.
