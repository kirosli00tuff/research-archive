## Summary — the strongest case for the thesis in this repository

This repository is the most disciplined in the programme: the criterion that closed H1 was committed and
pushed before a line of the measuring tool existed, and I verified that in the live history. The case for
the thesis is not concealment — it is that **three headline positives are each measured against something
other than the thing they are said to establish**. "E1 power 0.832450, a shortfall of 131 becomes a surplus
of 80" is the power of a one-sided test against a **zero-Sharpe null**; against the registered bar E1 must
actually clear — deflated Sharpe ≥ 1.00 — the same arithmetic gives **0.771972 on n = 875** and a minimum n
of **1,116**, i.e. a shortfall of 241. H1b's "the registered mechanism is confirmed" restates an input: the
scan's shift column is the registered cost constant, `0.030 + 0.030 × stop share`, exact on all five rows,
and the one independent check is degenerate at 0.0 on every floor. And the sign Amendment 21 called negative
(−0.001068) sits **inside one binomial standard error** of break-even and flips at a within-session
correlation of **ρ = 0.0029**. The closure that actually stands — `cost-bound` on a measured shift of
0.467065, 11.57× the central mean — survives every one of these objections, and the repository says so first.

### AUDIT-B-ORBExperiment-2
- **Repository / SHA:** ORBExperiment / f2aa675a80fc06b74979040b3028154d1add5ec0
- **Hypothesis or artifact:** H1 co-primary E2 (amended, Amendments 19/21) — the implied central mean 0.040386 and the deciding sign of −0.001068
- **Direction:** B
- **Class:** STATISTICAL
- **Severity:** S1
- **Label:** DERIVED
- **Claim challenged (verbatim quote):** "> ## **The measured pessimistic shift of 0.041455 USD/share EXCEEDS the implied central mean of 0.040386 at the registered 1.40 assumption.** / > ## **The pessimistic-cost mean is −0.001068. It is NEGATIVE.**" — ORBExperiment/progress.md:3244-3245 (the same two sentences at ORBExperiment/REGISTRATION.md:3358-3360, carried to ORBExperiment/README.md:118 and ORBExperiment/HYPOTHESES.md:82)
- **Evidence:**
  - The "implied central mean" is **not measured**. It is derived: `implied_mean = dispersion * daily / math.sqrt(k)` — ORBExperiment/tools/dispersion_compute.py:194 — where `daily = annual_sharpe / sqrt(252)` and `k` is trades per session. Inputs: dispersion **2.029585 USD/share on n = 550 priced trades** (ORBExperiment/progress.md:2698), `k = 550/28 = 19.642857` (ORBExperiment/reports/stage_a3rescue/task3_execution_mix.json, `trades_with_a_resolved_exit: 550`, `sessions_used: 28`).
  - The `/sqrt(k)` step is exactly the assumption that **trades within a session are independent**: it takes session PnL variance to be k·σ². **The registration denies that assumption in the section that defines the power method**: "the trades inside a session are not independent, they share the session's market factor, and E2's permutation clusters on session date precisely because of that" — ORBExperiment/REGISTRATION.md:534-536. E2's own test statistic is a session-clustered permutation: "by **permutation on session labels with trades clustered by session date**, so that **a single strong session cannot masquerade as many independent observations**" — ORBExperiment/REGISTRATION.md:448-451; implemented at ORBExperiment/src/orb/verdict.py:166-180.
  - The deciding margin is 0.040386 − 0.041455 = **−0.001068 USD/share, i.e. 2.6 percent of the central mean**. The repository records the fragility in the neighbouring sentence — "break-even Sharpe 1.437035, and the registered 1.40 sits 2.577 percent below it" (ORBExperiment/progress.md:3266-3267) — and treats both inputs as exact.
- **Recomputation:**
  - Method: under equicorrelation ρ among a session's k trades, session dispersion is σ·sqrt(k(1+(k−1)ρ)), so the per-trade mean implied by a given daily Sharpe is `implied_mean × sqrt(1+(k−1)ρ)`.
  - The repository's ρ = 0 reproduces **0.040386172** exactly (stored 0.040386172), confirming the identity. **ρ = 0.002876 — under three thousandths — makes the pessimistic-cost mean exactly zero.** At ρ = 0.01 the implied central mean is 0.043990 and the pessimistic-cost mean is **+0.002535**, i.e. positive, and E2's alternative is no longer inside its null.
  - **Stated against my own thesis, because it is true:** the *final* closure is robust to this. On the measured band (shift 0.467065) the correlation needed to flip the sign is **ρ = 7.12**, which is impossible; even at ρ = 1 the pessimistic-cost mean is **−0.288**. The finding therefore bears on Amendment 21's stated conclusion (progress.md Entries 31–32, REGISTRATION.md:3358-3360, README.md:118, HYPOTHESES.md:82), **not** on the `cost-bound` closure.
- **What would resolve it:** report the implied central mean with the within-session correlation stated as an explicit registered assumption and a sensitivity band, or derive it from a session-level dispersion measured directly — the sealed writer already reads whole sessions, so σ_session is computable inside it without a new blindness exception.
- **Confidence:** HIGH — the identity is one line of the repository's own code, the registration's own sentence contradicts the assumption it encodes, and the sensitivity is arithmetic reproduced at consolidation.
- **Could later commits invalidate this?:** no — the anchored SHA is live HEAD and Amendment 21's text is append-only.
- **Disclosed by authors?:** **no** — the repository discloses the dispersion's post-holdout regime caveat and the 2.577 percent break-even margin, but nowhere states that the implied central mean assumes within-session independence, and Section 9 asserts the opposite at REGISTRATION.md:534-536.
- **Merged from:** DRAFT-p3-progress-2. Attacks the same sentence as AUDIT-B-ORBExperiment-4 on **different** grounds (correlation in the `/√k` step, versus sampling error in the stop-exit share); neither subsumes the other and each survives if the other is rejected, so they are kept separate as the merge rule requires.
