## Leads for the cross-repository pass

Positives and artifacts here that another repository relies on, and standards applied here that look laxer or stricter than the house norm. Comparison repositories are my best guess, not a verified dependency.

1. **solclear Finding 2 is load-bearing in this repository and should be checked at its source.** `docs/ACCESS.md:200` — "solclear Finding 2 (manufactured survivorship) is why this is first-class" — and `REGISTRATION.md:192-194` — "solclear Finding 2 measured that mechanism at 60 points of death-rate gap". NPLExperiment's entire survivorship defence (row 6, and AUDIT-B-NPLExperiment-1) is motivated by that citation. **Check in solclear:** that Finding 2 exists, that "60 points of death-rate gap" is its figure with its n, and how large and how stratified solclear's own survivorship probe was. If solclear probed a broader or stratified sample, NPLExperiment's n = 6 is laxer than the standard it explicitly inherits. **Comparison repo: solclear.**
2. **The n = 6 data-availability probe as a cohort-validity certificate.** Every repository with a retrospective cohort drawn from a venue's served history needs an equivalent gate. **Check:** the size, stratification and reported confidence bound of the equivalent probe elsewhere. If the house norm is 20+ or a stratified probe, this is a laxer standard; if the house norm is also ~6, the finding is program-level rather than repo-level. **Comparison repos: HFExperiment, InsiderExperiment, ETFExperiment.**
3. **Multiplicity corrected everywhere, dependence corrected nowhere.** Sidak across co-primaries (1 − 0.95^(1/3) = 0.016952), Sidak-within across cells, a familywise cull across hypotheses, an exact two-look alpha split — and no cluster, design-effect or permutation treatment anywhere, under a design that reuses controls with replacement and overlaps every holding window on one venue. **Check:** whether any sibling register models dependence. If none does, this is a house-wide norm and belongs in a program-level finding rather than as an NPL defect. Start where the overlap is worst. **Comparison repos: all Experiment repos; first PMExperiment (same anchor date), then ORBExperiment and VRPExperiment (intraday, heavily overlapping windows).**
4. **ADR-017 — "the generating-sample-derived end governs the cull; adopting the optimistic end after seeing the pessimistic one would be outcome-fitting" (DECISIONS.md:206-210).** Applied to H3 and H4, not to H2. **Check:** whether the same principle is stated in sibling registers, and whether it is applied symmetrically there — in particular to whichever hypothesis survived. **Comparison repos: NPDExperiment (nearest naming and design sibling), PMExperiment.**
