## Summary — the strongest case for the thesis in this repository

The case is not arithmetic. Every derivable figure I recomputed is correct, and the note's
scope limits are unusually strong and placed before the evidence. The case is the gap between
the guarantees this repository *ships* and what they actually cover. Both flagship honesty
detectors pass vacuously in two hollowing modes, one detector has no canary at all, and
README.md, DECISIONS.md, progress.md and the test module's own docstring each assert that every
detector is canaried. README.md — the document a reader meets first — sits entirely outside the
drift machinery (it carries no generated marker at all, so 0 % of its numeric tokens are

presents a comparison the note explicitly retracts.
The note asserts a population of hypotheses it never enumerates, and compares 4 power
*repositories* against 3 contrast *repositories* carrying at least 11 closed hypotheses (the
per-hypothesis count is disclosed in the note's own contrast section, which is why that finding
is capped at S3). Three further self-descriptions — "one derived figure", "every figure mapped
to its source", "111 tests" — are each false at the anchor.
