## The strongest case for the thesis in this repository

This is one of the most disciplined repositories in the program, and that is what makes the pattern
legible: eleven alpha hypotheses closed against pre-registered bars, with Benjamini–Hochberg, deflated
Sharpe ratios, survivorship-free universes and an adversarial self-audit whose every correction moved
against the authors. The over-claim sits exactly where those controls stop. The three things the
project presents as positives — the shipped hard-rug clearance scorer, the D2 behavioural finding, and
H1's "AUC 0.596 says there is signal there" reopening clause — carry no interval, no multiplicity
correction and no selection correction, and the mechanism by which they escaped is structural
(ADR-044 puts the detection track off the register). The scorer's headline **0.984** is 63 correct
clearances and **one** error, at a threshold chosen on the fold it is scored on, at a base rate its own
scope note says is the wrong one (0.353 at the wild rate it names); D2's deciding lift is **+0.032 ±
0.039** from an uncorrected 18-cell scan; and H1's registered reopening threshold of 3 bps is derived
from a figure the same register marks superseded, against six-day measurements that miss it by 4–103×.
Where the repository's own audit *did* correct something, three of the remediations it announced never
reached the code or the artifact they were announced for.
